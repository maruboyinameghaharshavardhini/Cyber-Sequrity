Common Cyber Threats

Understanding common cyber threats is the first step toward protecting yourself online.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to steal information.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software that can damage systems or steal information.

🔒

Ransomware

Malware that can encrypt files and demand payment.

🎭

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or taking unsafe actions.

🔑

Password Attacks

Attempts to guess, steal or reuse passwords.

👤

Identity Theft

Using stolen personal information for fraudulent activities.

About CyberShield

🛡️ What is CyberShield?

CyberShield is a cyber security awareness project designed to help people understand common online threats and develop safer digital habits.

🎯 Our Goal

The goal of this project is to make cyber security easier to understand through simple explanations, interactive quizzes and phishing awareness tests.
